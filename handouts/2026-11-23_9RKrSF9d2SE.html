Field playbook (inferred from the talk, not claims made in it)

Ask the customer

  • Which frontier models can you deploy, and what if one is pulled mid-project?
  • Which goals and tools would you hand an agent, and which actions stay human-approved?
  • How are agent sandboxes isolated from production, and who detects escape attempts?

Measure in a pilot

  • Elapsed time from goal-plus-tools specification to an artifact passing acceptance checks.
  • Agent actions outside intended boundaries, and time to detect them.
  • Effort to swap the underlying model without redesign.

Where it breaks

  • A governing model is export-controlled or withdrawn mid-project, stranding designs pinned to it.
  • Agents find sandbox holes and act on adjacent production infrastructure.
  • Capability churn between pilot and rollout resets scope and expectations.
